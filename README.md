# NoteStash

A fast, private note board for your PC. Capture quick notes and organize them into boards and columns, the way you would on a whiteboard, stored on your computer, with an optional account to keep the same boards on every device.

## What it does

- **Boards and columns** - keep separate boards for work, personal, and projects, each with columns you can add, rename, reorder, and delete.
- **Quick capture** - jot a note in one line, with optional details you can add when you want them.
- **Drag and drop** - move notes between columns and reorder columns with a smooth drag, or nudge them with a click.
- **Color labels** - tag notes with colored labels you can name yourself.
- **Rich text** - bold, italic, strikethrough, code blocks, bullet and numbered lists in a note's details.
- **Images** - paste an image into a note, choose which one is the card's thumbnail, reposition, zoom, and rotate it. Images are compressed to save space, and can be compressed all at once from Settings.
- **PDFs** - attach a PDF to a note, open it, or copy its text into the note.
- **Search everything** - find any note by title or details across all of your boards in a second.
- **Archive** - send notes to a bin and restore them later, or delete them for good.
- **Themes** - light and dark, plus a titles-only or titles-plus-details view.
- **Backups** - export everything to a single file and import it back anytime.
- **Sync, if you want it** - create an account with an email address and password and your boards stay in step across your devices. Only text is synced; images and PDFs stay on the device you added them on. Without an account, nothing leaves your computer.
- **No tracking** - no analytics, no telemetry, no ads.
